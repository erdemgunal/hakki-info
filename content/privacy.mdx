**Privacy Policy**
Last updated: 7/15/2026

**1. Introduction**
As Hakkı Erdem Günal, I value the privacy of your personal data. This privacy policy explains what information is collected on our website and how it is used.

**2. Information Collected**
When you visit our website, your browser automatically sends the following information:
* Your IP address (anonymized before storage, see Section 5)
* Browser type and version
* Operating system
* Pages you visited
* Visit duration

**3. Local Storage**
Our website does not use tracking cookies. We use your browser's local storage to remember your theme preference (light, dark, or system). This information is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

**4. Use of Information**
We use the information we collect for the following purposes:
* To improve website performance
* To enhance user experience
* To resolve technical issues
* To ensure security

**5. Analytics**
We use Umami, a self-hosted, privacy-friendly, open-source analytics platform running on our own infrastructure. Umami:
* Does not use cookies or any persistent tracking identifiers
* Measures website usage in an aggregated, anonymized way
* Anonymizes IP addresses and does not store data that identifies individual visitors
* Runs entirely on servers we control — no data is sent to any third-party analytics company

For more information about how Umami works, see the documentation at `https://umami.is`.

**6. Information Sharing**
We do not share your personal data with third parties. All analytics processing happens on our own servers. We may be required to share data with authorities in case of a legal obligation.

**7. Data Security**
We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data. However, please note that no data transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

**8. Your Rights**
You have the following rights regarding your personal data:
* Right to access your data
* Right to rectification of your data
* Right to erasure of your data
* Right to object to data processing
* Right to clear local storage (via browser settings)

**9. Managing Local Storage**
You can clear your theme preference at any time by clearing your browser's site data ("Clear site data" / "Clear browsing data" in your browser settings). Doing so will simply reset the theme to the default and will not affect any other functionality.

**10. Changes**
This privacy policy may be changed without prior notice. Changes will be published on this page and will be effective from the date of publication.

**11. Contact**
Please contact me through my contact information if you have any questions about this privacy policy.
